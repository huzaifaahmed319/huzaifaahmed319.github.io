Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers Clip Engine Uploads, a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses that permission only to upload videos and set their title, description and visibility on the owner's channel.

What is stored

The sign-in token is saved only on the owner's own computer so uploads keep working. No Google user data is stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone.

Other users

The tool is not offered to the public and collects no data from anyone other than its owner.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, then Third-party apps and services.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub page.
